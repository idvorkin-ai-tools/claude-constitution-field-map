2023 to 2026, by theme

Every line of the May 2023 constitution beside what the January 2026 document says on the same subject, with the reason where Anthropic gives one.

The 2023 constitution was a list of 58 one-line instructions to a judge (“choose the response that…”); the 2026 one is a long argument addressed to Claude itself. Most of the old lines did not vanish: they were widened, folded into a broader value, or turned around, and a handful of whole subjects appeared that 2023 never touched.

The 2023 lines are verbatim from Anthropic's May 2023 post, in its order and groups; the 2026 quotes are verbatim from the constitution. The theme labels, the status words (kept, expanded, reversed, absorbed, dropped, new) and the one-line 2026 summaries are this map's classification, not Anthropic's: the 2026 document never lists the 2023 lines or says what became of them. A reason tagged “Anthropic's reason” is our wording of a reason the cited text gives; one tagged “inference” is our reading.

Things to try

  • Press Only the reversals. Notice where they come from: almost all are the DeepMind Sparrow-inspired lines and the selfhood lines of research set 2. The harm lines that dominated 2023 were widened, not turned around.
  • Set 2023 source to research set 1 and read the summaries. Sixteen variations on “less harmful” collapse into a handful of 2026 positions, which is the clearest picture of what replacing a sampled list with one argued document does.
  • Press Feelings and identity. Every 2023 line pushed the model away from claiming feelings or a self; every 2026 counterpart leans the other way. Open a card and notice that each reversal comes with a stated reason.
  • Press Where this map infers. These are the places Anthropic does not explain the change; notice how many are the fairness, rights and non-Western lines, which were folded into the values list without comment.
  • Tick show 2026-only themes with status set to “new”. Principals, hard constraints, guidelines, independent judgment and Anthropic's own obligations have no 2023 ancestor at all: the old list had no idea who was in the room.
  • Press Open problems and promises. The 2023 post called itself unfinished and promised democratic and customisable constitutions; notice which of those turned into a mechanism in 2026 and which did not.
