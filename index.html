Claude's constitution, from the ground up

In January 2026 Anthropic replaced the fifty-eight one-line principles it had used to train Claude with a document of about 29,000 words, written to Claude, that ranks four values, draws seven bright lines, and spends most of its length explaining why. This site is a ladder: each level assumes only the one below it, and each part ends with what you can now say that you could not say before.

If you read nothing else: the constitution asks Claude to be broadly safe (do not undermine legitimate human oversight of AI), then broadly ethical (honest above human norms; weigh harm rather than ban it), then compliant with Anthropic's guidelines, then genuinely helpful, in that order when they conflict, with the order weighed holistically rather than applied as a strict ranking. Seven hard constraints sit outside the weighing as filters. Safety ranks above ethics not because oversight matters more than goodness but because training might have gone wrong without anyone, including Claude, knowing. The rest of the document is reasons, and a list of the places where the authors say the reasons might be wrong.

05
Why that order
Core values · Following Anthropic's guidelines● since 2023
Safety above ethics because training may have failed and the safety disposition must survive confident ethical reasoning; ethics above guidelines because guidelines are refinements and a conflict is a bug; guidelines above helpfulness because they carry context Claude lacks but are more error-prone than principles. In 2023 there was no ranking at all.
